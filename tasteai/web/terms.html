What the current app does

Menu photos and optional AI processing

Only upload menu images you are authorized to share. When an operator enables external OCR, TasteAI sends the uploaded image to the configured AI provider for extraction. The extracted text and prices are proposals, may be wrong, and do not become approved menu evidence until a reviewer checks and accepts them. Optional query understanding and grounded menu-claim selection can send the relevant query and bounded approved evidence fields to the configured provider; optional embeddings can send approved menu text. These features have separate opt-ins, are disabled by default, and are subject to the provider's terms. AI output is not evidence and cannot establish facts absent from approved evidence.
